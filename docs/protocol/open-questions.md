Prefixes are the same as in `meshmapper-app-notes.md` (`MMA/` app @`3b5b5af`, `MMW/` wiki @`83c74c7`, `MC/`
MeshCore @`81dbae59`) and `observer-firmware-notes.md` (`OBS/` @`a714b38`). **"Inferred"** marks anything not
stated in code or docs.

| 8 | Backbone formula | A **link** = two hops adjacent in an observer-logged path; each packet scores **1 point per link once**, however many observers logged it. Final hop into the observer is not a link. A repeater's points = sum over its links; **share** = points ÷ region total. **Backbone** = smallest busiest-first set reaching **50 %** (the one crossing the line is included). Links unseen for 7 days are dropped (admin setting). A link scores only if both hops resolve to exactly one placed, active repeater on the same preset, not co-located, ≤ 250 km apart. Points update about hourly. Trunks = each backbone site's 3 busiest links to other backbone sites | `MMW/backbone.md:3`, `:11–37`, `:41–74`, `:81–93`, `:104–109`, `:139–141` | No |
| 9 | Leaderboard edge cases | Documented: refreshed once a day (up to 24 h lag); top 10 per board; 1 point per ping + 1.5 per verified DEFER square (once per square per session); Explorers = distinct 300 m squares; Pioneers = first in a square, forever; devices merged per linked account; unnamed/Anonymous devices shown but not ranked (and skipped for Ping Leader); repeater ping counts exclude RX-only; ranges > 500 km ignored; ambiguous-ID pings not credited; private repeaters masked; regions with dup detection off show a warning and are left off global repeater boards | `MMW/leaderboards.md:5`, `:20–37`, `:43–54`, `:70`; `MMW/awards.md:37`, `:49` | Partly: **tie-breaking**, the **time zone and boundary of the 7-day window**, and whether deleted pings are subtracted are not documented. Not needed: our board (M8) defines its own rules |
| 10 | Companion signature format (device linking) | MeshCore companion signing: `CMD_SIGN_START` (33) → reply with max length (u32 LE; 8 KB); `CMD_SIGN_DATA` (34) chunks (MeshMapper sends ≤ 128 B each, one OK per chunk); `CMD_SIGN_FINISH` (35) → `RESP_CODE_SIGNATURE` + **64-byte Ed25519 signature over the raw bytes** with the node key. MeshMapper's portal sends a 32-byte random nonce (64 hex, 2-minute expiry); the app checks the shape and signs the **raw 32 bytes, never the hex text** | `MC/examples/companion_radio/MyMesh.cpp:41–43`, `:142`, `:1781–1812`; `MMA/services/meshcore/connection.dart:3318–3360`; `MMA/providers/app_state_provider.dart:12971–12991`; `MMW/portal.md:52–60` | No for the format. Our own challenge shape (length, domain prefix, expiry) is an M8 design choice |

## What goes to Andy

Only one question needs him, and it is optional:

- **Q4 (CMH region settings on MeshMapper).** Do we want to mirror CMH's MeshMapper region policy (TX slots, flood
  on/off, minimum interval) in MeshSonar? If yes, Andy reads the values from his own app's Connect screen or asks the
  CMH region admin. If no, the question closes.

Q3 is proposed for closing as moot. Q2 (status-4 internals) and Q9 (tie-breaking) are closed-source details we do not
need, because CMAT computes its own status and board.
